About

Design starts with understanding.

Product Designer · 4+ years · South Florida

I'm a Product Designer with 4+ years of experience across healthcare, SaaS, mobile apps, and dashboards.

Before design, I spent about six years in customer care. Listening to people describe their problems, often in frustrated, imperfect words, taught me to find the real issue underneath. That's still where my design work starts.

I like understanding how a business actually runs, untangling complicated requirements, and turning them into products people can use without thinking twice.

How I work

I'd rather understand the problem than guess at the screen.

I start with conversations and the systems people already use, then map the rules and flows before designing interfaces. I work closely with founders, product managers, and developers, and I prototype early so we can react to something real.

AI is part of how I explore requirements and test product logic. Product design is still the job.
