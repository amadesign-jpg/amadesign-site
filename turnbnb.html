Overview

One product, three roles, a lot of rules.

TurnBnB connects short-term rental Hosts with cleaning Team Leaders and the Cleaners on their teams. Hosts add properties and connect booking calendars. Team Leaders grow their business. Cleaners do the work.

The hardest part wasn't any single screen. It was the rules between the roles.

Requirements kept evolving, so before designing screens I mapped roles, permissions, and relationships into one product model, and checked every flow against it.

My role

As the UX/UI Designer, I translated evolving client requirements into structured mobile experiences, from user flows and permissions to high-fidelity UI and interactive prototypes.

01 / Host invitation

Helping Team Leaders bring their existing Hosts into TurnBnB.

Many Team Leaders already clean for Hosts outside TurnBnB. Inviting them means two roles in one flow: the Team Leader starts it, and the Host joins, creates the property, and connects it to the Team Leader.

I mapped the invitation flow across both roles before designing the individual screens.

Longer screens scroll inside their frames, like the app.

02 / Subscriptions & access

Monetizing growth without creating dead ends.

Team Leader plans can limit markets, properties, visibility to Hosts, and team size. The client also wanted Team Leaders to buy one more market or property without upgrading their whole plan.

Instead of designing a different paywall for every restriction, I created one decision pattern that could work across multiple triggers.

  • Passive

    Visibility

    Free Team Leaders are hidden from Hosts. The app explains it without blocking anything.

  • High intent

    Property access

    Appears when a Team Leader opens a restricted property or tries to contact a Host.

  • Growth

    Cleaner growth

    Appears only when a Team Leader adds another cleaner.

One pattern, multiple triggers. The same decision supports property, market, and cleaner restrictions, so Team Leaders learn it once and always see a way forward.

Market selection

Start with what's free.

The first market establishes value before any limit appears. Local demand shows up the moment a market is selected.

Manage markets

Make limits visible.

Plan usage sits at the top of the screen, so a Team Leader sees the limit before reaching it, with Upgrade one tap away.

Add one

Offer a proportional next step.

A Team Leader who needs one more market or property can add exactly that, without upgrading the entire plan. The add-on works right away.

Upgrade

Compare plans, then confirm what changes.

Plans sit side by side with their limits. Before confirming, the summary shows what the new plan includes next to current usage.

Scaling down

Leaving a limit is as clear as reaching one.

Removing an add-on states exactly when it ends. If a downgrade leaves a Team Leader over the new limits, the required add-ons are applied for them, with no blocker screen.

Prices and plan names shown are placeholders.

Mobile UI

One product, tailored to each role.

Hosts, Team Leaders, and Cleaners share the same navigation, cards, forms, and status colors. What changes is the information and the actions each role sees.
